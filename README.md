## 📌 Task Objective
The objective of Task 2 is to perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** to uncover patterns, trends, and relationships within the dataset and translate them into actionable business insights.

---

## 📊 Dataset Description
The dataset used for this task represents **customer purchase behavior**, including:
- Customer demographics (age, gender)
- Purchase amount and product category
- City-level information
- Payment methods and purchase dates

This dataset supports customer segmentation and sales trend analysis.

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Descriptive Statistics
- Dataset structure and data types
- Summary statistics for numerical variables
- Frequency analysis for categorical variables

### 2. Univariate Analysis
- Distribution of purchase amounts
- Purchase frequency across product categories

### 3. Business-Oriented Analysis
The following business questions were addressed:
- Which product category generates the highest revenue?
- Which city contributes the most to total sales?
- What payment methods are most preferred?

### 4. Multivariate Analysis
- Relationship between customer age and purchase amount
- Correlation analysis using heatmaps

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Electronics products have the highest average purchase value
- Delhi and Bangalore are the top-performing cities
- UPI and Cash are the most commonly used payment methods
- Customers aged 30–45 tend to make higher-value purchases
- A moderate correlation exists between age and spending
